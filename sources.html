Sources and Attribution

This page explains where the reader's Quran data comes from and how it differs from YouOOO's original study material.

Reader data supplied by Quran.com

The interactive reader requests chapter metadata, Uthmani-script Quran text, available translations, tafsir resources, and recitation metadata from the Quran.com API. Audio files are delivered through the Quran.com audio content service. Provider availability determines which editions and reciters appear in the controls.

Translation and tafsir text remains the work of the translator, commentator, publisher, or data provider named by the reader. YouOOO does not claim authorship of that material. The source label shown after an ayah is selected identifies the chosen tafsir resource.

Original YouOOO editorial material

Introductions, theme summaries, vocabulary notes, study prompts, navigation explanations, and learning guidance marked as part of a YouOOO study guide are original editorial material. They are concise learning aids, not translations, legal rulings, or replacements for qualified scholarship.

Method and care

Study guides stay close to the chapter's visible structure and widely recognized themes. Verse references are included when a prompt depends on a particular passage. Claims that require specialist judgment are avoided, and readers are encouraged to compare translations and consult qualified teachers for detailed interpretation or recitation correction.

Reporting an issue

Quran text deserves careful handling. If you notice a display, attribution, translation-label, audio, or verse-reference problem, please send the page URL and details through the contact page. Third-party data corrections may also need to be reported to the named provider.
